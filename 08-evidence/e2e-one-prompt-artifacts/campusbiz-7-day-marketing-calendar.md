# Hostel Crumb Co. · 7-Day Launch Marketing Calendar

**Product:** Homemade cookies (4 flavours)
**Price:** RM5.00 / box (fixed — all channels must use this exact price)
**Daily capacity:** Up to 40 boxes/day (never advertise more)
**Order cutoff:** 14:00 daily
**Delivery window:** 16:00 – 19:00
**Payment:** DuitNow (QR / transfer) on confirmation
**Contact / CTA:** WhatsApp +60 12-345 6789
**Tone:** Warm, homemade, friendly student voice

> **Compliance rule for every post:** RM5.00/box only. "Up to 40 boxes a day" only. **Never** mention income, earnings, profit, or a RM6k/month target — those are not part of this plan and are not achievable at this price and capacity.

---

## Day 1 — Opening teaser · Build awareness
- **Channel:** Hostel WhatsApp groups (first post) + Instagram Story
- **Goal:** Let the cluster know a new homemade cookie business is opening; collect early interest.
- **Copy:**
```text
Hi everyone! 🍪 Hostel Crumb Co. is opening right here on campus.

Freshly baked homemade cookies — baked in small batches and delivered straight to your hostel block.
Price: RM5.00 per box. Delivery 16:00–19:00.

We bake up to 40 boxes a day, so we'll go until they're gone.
Drop a 🍪 in the chat if you want first pick when orders open tomorrow!
```

## Channel notes
- **Hostel WhatsApp groups:** primary conversion channel — always include RM5.00, 14:00 cutoff, 16:00–19:00 delivery.
- **Instagram Stories:** reach + social proof — use link sticker to the landing page, polls, and delivery photos.
- **In-person tasting:** trust-building only — do not make health or income claims.
- **Landing page:** link from Instagram bio and every Story CTA; it restates RM5.00/box and "up to 40 boxes a day".

## Claims guardrail (do not cross)
- ❌ No income / earnings / profit claims (e.g. no "earn RM6k a month").
- ❌ No promise above **40 boxes/day** or beyond the **RM5.00** price.
- ❌ No health or "best cookies on campus" absolute claims that can't be verified.
- ✅ Only what the plan supports: RM5.00/box, up to 40 boxes/day, 14:00 cutoff, 16:00–19:00 delivery, DuitNow payment.
